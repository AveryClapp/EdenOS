# EdenOS

> *Total visibility. Zero noise.*

---

Most people don't have a time problem. They have a decision problem.

EdenOS is a personal operating system built for individuals managing too many high-stakes things at once — research, engineering, athletics, career, creative work — who are tired of knowing what matters but not knowing what to do *right now*.

It doesn't just schedule. It reasons.

---

## What it does

Eden maintains a living model of everything you're working toward — goals, projects, tasks, deadlines, dependencies — and uses it to make intelligent scheduling decisions on your behalf. Silently, in the background, continuously.

When you open it, it tells you what to do. When you ask why, it explains. When things change, it adapts.

- **Goal Graph** — every task linked to a milestone linked to a long-term goal. Priority is never arbitrary.
- **Auto-Scheduler** — constrained optimization across your real availability, cognitive load, and energy state.
- **Eden Intelligence** — an LLM reasoning layer with full context injection. Not a chatbot. An advisor.
- **Adaptive Learning** — duration estimates, energy curves, and priority weights improve from your actual behavior over time.
- **Temporal Decay** — urgency compounds nonlinearly as deadlines approach. No manual reprioritization required.
- **Recovery Modeling** — physical training load affects cognitive scheduling. The system knows when to push and when to back off.

Integrates with Google Calendar, Outlook, and GitHub. Treats them all as inputs.

---

## Philosophy

The system is designed around one insight: the cost of deciding is often higher than the cost of doing. Eden eliminates the decision. You just act.

It is opinionated, personal, and built for one user. It gets smarter the longer it runs.
